# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/shadcn-labs/mdxcn/security/advisories/new). Do not open a public issue or discussion.

Include the affected registry item or route, when you copied the source, steps to reproduce, and the potential impact. Do not include credentials or unrelated private data.

We will investigate legitimate reports and work to fix confirmed problems. mdxcn ships copied source, not an npm package; consumers must apply fixes to their own copies.
